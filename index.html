Common questions

Do you work with AEM as a Cloud Service and older AEM 6.5 sites?

Yes, I've worked hands-on with both. If your setup is unusual, we'll cover it on the intro call.

Do I need to give you access to our systems?

For development and fix work, yes: typically an AEM author account and repository access under your usual security process. An accessibility audit can often start with just your public URLs (or a staging login).

Will an audit make us "WCAG compliant"?

An audit tells you exactly where you stand against WCAG 2.2 AA and what to fix, in priority order. Compliance comes from fixing the issues and keeping them fixed, which I can help with. It's not a legal certification or legal advice.

Can you work alongside our agency or in-house developers?

Yes. I'm used to working inside larger teams and fitting into existing Git workflows, sprint processes and code review.

How quickly can you start?

Usually within one to two weeks of an agreed scope. Retainer clients get priority turnaround.
